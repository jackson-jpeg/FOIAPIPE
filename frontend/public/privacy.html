Privacy

FOIA Archive is an internal tool for one small team. It is parked for now: the staff side is offline and nobody can sign in.

What this site keeps

The sign-in page sets no cookies and keeps nothing in your browser while the tool is parked. When the staff side is running, signing in keeps one sign-in token in local storage (foiaarchive_token), which is strictly necessary for sign-in. Fonts are served from foiaarchive.com itself.

Visitor counts

Only the public page (/public) is counted, with Umami running on our own server, so nothing goes to an analytics company. It sets no cookies and does not follow you to other sites. For each visit it records the page, the referring site, your browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and an approximate location worked out from your IP address (the address itself is not stored). Visit records are deleted after 24 months. Every visitor is treated the same way whether or not the browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Search engines

This site asks search engines not to list any of its pages.
